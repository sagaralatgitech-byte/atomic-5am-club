## 📖 How to Use

### 1️⃣ Start Your Morning Routine

1. Navigate to the **Morning** tab
2. Complete each 20-minute block:
   - **MOVE**: Enter what exercise you did
   - **REFLECT**: Note your reflection practice and fill in Daily Five targets
   - **GROW**: Record what you learned
3. Check off each section as you complete it

### 2️⃣ Plan Your Day with Time Blocking

1. Go to the **Schedule** tab
2. Click "Add Block" to create time blocks
3. Set time, duration, and activity for each block
4. Choose category (Deep Work, Exercise, Break, etc.)
5. Click "Export to Calendar" when done

### 3️⃣ Get Phone Alerts

1. After planning your schedule, click **"Export to Calendar"**
2. Open the downloaded `.ics` file on your phone
3. Import into Google Calendar (or your preferred calendar app)
4. Enable notifications in your calendar settings
5. You'll now get alerts 10-15 minutes before each block!

### 4️⃣ Track Your Habits

1. Visit the **Habits** tab
2. Click "Add Habit" to create new habits
3. For each habit, enter:
   - Habit name (e.g., "Exercise for 30 minutes")
   - Two-minute version (e.g., "Put on workout clothes")
   - Category
4. Check off habits as you complete them
5. Watch your streaks grow!

### 5️⃣ Define Your Identity

1. Go to **Identity** tab
2. Write who you want to become
3. See how each completed habit is a vote for your new identity

### 6️⃣ Stack Your Habits

1. Navigate to **Stacking** tab
2. Create habit stacks using the formula:
   - "After [current habit], I will [new habit]"
3. Example: "After I brush my teeth, I will floss"

### 7️⃣ Practice Gratitude

1. Open **Gratitude** tab
2. Write three things you're grateful for
3. Do this daily during your Reflect period

## 🎓 The Science Behind It

### From Atomic Habits by James Clear

- **1% Better Every Day**: Small improvements compound into remarkable results
- **Identity-Based Habits**: Focus on who you want to become, not what you want to achieve
- **Four Laws of Behavior Change**:
  1. Make it Obvious
  2. Make it Attractive
  3. Make it Easy
  4. Make it Satisfying
- **Never Miss Twice**: Missing once is okay; missing twice starts a new (bad) habit
- **Habit Stacking**: Link new habits to existing ones for automatic execution

### From The 5 AM Club by Robin Sharma

- **20/20/20 Formula**: Transform your mornings with structured 60-minute routine
- **Victory Hour**: The first hour sets the tone for your entire day
- **90/90/1 Rule**: Deep work on priority projects builds excellence
- **60/10 Method**: Work-rest cycles maintain peak performance
- **66 Days to Automaticity**: Commit for 66 days to make habits automatic
- **10 Tactics of Lifelong Genius**: Proven methods from billionaires and top performers

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Maximum Success

1. **Wake up at 4:45 AM** - Gives you time to get ready for 5 AM Victory Hour
2. **Prepare the night before** - Lay out workout clothes, prep journal, set up coffee
3. **Go to bed by 10 PM** - Get 8 hours of quality sleep
4. **Start small with Two-Minute Rule** - Make habits ridiculously easy to start
5. **Track everything** - What gets measured gets improved
6. **Never miss twice** - One slip is okay; two creates a pattern
7. **Review weekly** - Check Weekly Goals tab every Sunday
8. **Celebrate small wins** - Each completed habit is progress

### Troubleshooting

**Q: I can't wake up at 5 AM**
- Start by waking up 15 minutes earlier each week
- Ensure you're getting 7-8 hours of sleep
- Go to bed at the same time every night
- Avoid screens 1 hour before bed

**Q: I missed a day**
- Don't worry! Never miss twice is the rule
- Get back on track immediately
- One missed day doesn't break your progress

**Q: Calendar export not working**
- Ensure you filled in times for your blocks
- Try downloading on your phone instead of computer
- Check that your calendar app is installed

**Q: Data not saving**
- Data saves automatically to your browser
- Don't clear browser data/cookies
- For multi-device sync, consider the premium version (coming soon)
